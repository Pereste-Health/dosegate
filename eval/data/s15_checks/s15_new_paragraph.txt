**S15. Behaviors checked with the built binary:** Each behavior below was rerun against the version 2.3 binary built from commit f305cff, using minimal requests with opaque identifiers (m1, m2), only MedicationRequest and Medication resources, and references in the Type/id or urn:uuid:id form. The requests, outputs, and commands are in /Volumes/T7/tmp-dg/s15_checks/ (run_log.jsonl lists every command and output). Superseded orders. Version 2.3 fails closed on any order that carries priorPrescription, so a replacement order never prints and never closes the order it replaces. Under the default settings, every output for the patient is blocked. For lisinopril 20 mg once a day replacing lisinopril 10 mg twice a day, an output that names both orders is blocked with ambiguousDrug, an output that names one order is blocked with omittedOrder, and the fallback is blocked too. Under --allow-omissions, the exact reference form (MedicationRequest/m2) blocks any output that names either order, with ambiguousDrug when the names are the same and missingField when they differ, because the referenced order also fails closed. The urn:uuid and bare forms block the same outputs, except that when the names differ (amlodipine 5 mg once a day replacing lisinopril 10 mg twice a day), an output that names only the old order prints "Take lisinopril 10 mg by mouth twice a day.", so the superseded dose reaches the patient. An output with no slot is released under --allow-omissions in every form, so "Here is your medicine list." can reach the patient with neither order named. By default the same output is blocked with omittedOrder. Held and stopped orders. An order with doNotPerform true, or with status on-hold or stopped, is left out of released text when no slot names it. With an active metformin order (500 mg twice a day) and a warfarin order with doNotPerform true, the render path prints "Here is your medicine list. Take metformin 500 mg by mouth twice a day." under both the default settings and --allow-omissions, and the fallback prints "Take metformin 500 mg by mouth, twice a day." The same holds when the warfarin order has status on-hold or stopped. A slot that names a held or stopped order is blocked with inactiveOrder under --allow-omissions ("MedicationRequest m2 is not active"), and under the default settings the output is blocked with omittedOrder because the active metformin order is not named. Reported orders. An order with reportedBoolean true, or with any reportedReference key, has no usable dose, timing, or route. Under the default settings it blocks every output for the patient: a template that names only the active metformin order is blocked with omittedOrder, and the fallback is blocked with missingField ("MedicationRequest m2 has no usable sig"). Under --allow-omissions, the metformin-only template releases "Take metformin 500 mg by mouth twice a day.", a template with no slot is released, and a template that names the reported order is blocked with missingField ("MedicationRequest m2 has no usable dose"). Coded timing and free text. Under the default settings, an order with free text (a note, or dosage text, additionalInstruction, or patientInstruction) is blocked with missingField. Under --ignore-free-text, the structured fields are read and the free text is ignored. A structured acetaminophen 325 mg order with dosage text "Every four to six hours (qualifier value)" and a timing frequency of four a day prints "Take acetaminophen 325 mg by mouth four times a day." and, with a daily slot, "Take acetaminophen 325 mg by mouth four times a day, which is 1300 mg a day." The 455 normalized "Every four to six hours" orders were rerun on the Synthea bundles in /Volumes/T7/hearth-datasets/synthea/bundles_v1/fhir (the bundles are not in the repository), with the construction in eval/coded_timing_check.py. As recorded, all 455 are blocked with missingField under --ignore-free-text, because each keeps a category with a text and a requester written as a search query, which version 2.3 fails closed on. With category, requester, encounter, authoredOn, and meta removed, all 455 print under --ignore-free-text: 376 as "Take acetaminophen ... four times a day, which is 1300 mg a day." and 79 as "... four times a day, which is 1600 mg a day.", and none print without the flag. The 455 count therefore describes orders with those five fields removed. None of the 193 raw "Every twelve hours as required" orders prints under the option (all are blocked with missingField), and their structured forms are blocked too, with "MedicationRequest m1 has no usable drug", because the drug name 12 hr hydrocodone bitartrate is not accepted. A structured order with an accepted name does print as a scheduled dose: an acetaminophen 500 mg order with that dosage text, asNeededBoolean false, and a frequency of two a day prints "Take acetaminophen 500 mg by mouth twice a day." under the option. Weekly and as-needed lines. A weekly order with no daily slot prints "Take methotrexate 10 mg by mouth once a week." through the render path and "Take methotrexate 10 mg by mouth, once a week." through the fallback, and the line does not say that the dose is not for daily use. A template with a daily slot is blocked with missingField ("MedicationRequest m1 has no usable daily"). An as-needed order prints "Take oxycodone hydrochloride 5 mg by mouth once a day as needed." through the render path and "Take oxycodone hydrochloride 5 mg by mouth, once a day as needed." through the fallback. An acetaminophen 500 mg order with four doses a day and asNeededBoolean true prints "Take acetaminophen 500 mg by mouth four times a day as needed." through the render path and "Take acetaminophen 500 mg by mouth, four times a day as needed." through the fallback. An as-needed order whose reason is coded (asNeededCodeableConcept with a text) is blocked with missingField on both paths. Insulin. A structured Humulin R order of 20 units three times a day with the subcutaneous route prints "Take humulin r 20 units as a shot under the skin three times a day." on both paths; the name prints in lowercase, as the RxNorm lexicon holds it. The names "insulin regular" and "insulin, regular, human" are blocked with missingField ("MedicationRequest m1 has no usable drug"). The Humulin R U-100 label directs injection about 30 minutes before meals, which the line does not state, so a deployment must add that instruction after checking the label for the dispensed product. Basal and mealtime insulin, insulin glargine 20 units once a day with insulin lispro 6 units three times a day, returns ambiguousDrug on both paths, so the patient gets no text by either path. Metformin. The fallback prints "Take metformin 500 mg by mouth, twice a day." with a comma, and the render path prints "Take metformin 500 mg by mouth twice a day." without one. Counts. A dose written as a count (unit {tablet}) is blocked with missingField ("MedicationRequest m1 has no usable dose"). References. An encounter written as a search query (Encounter?identifier=abc|1) or as a fragment (#enc1) makes the order fail closed, and the render path blocks with missingField; the search-query form also blocks the fallback.
